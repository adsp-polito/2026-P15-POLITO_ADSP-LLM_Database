db_11 - Airline operations (Difficulty: Hard)
=============================================

Domain
------
The operations database of a fictional short/medium-haul carrier (flight prefix ZQ) based at
Rome Fiumicino (FCO), Milan Malpensa (MXP) and Naples (NAP). It links the fleet, its
maintenance history, the flight crew, the operated flight legs and the engine trend data
recorded in flight. All dates are UTC, in November 2026 for operations and during 2026 for
maintenance.

Collections
-----------
1. aircraft (4 docs) - fleet register. _id is a prefixed string ("AC-4101" ... "AC-4104").
   Holds registration, type, MSN, engines, MTOW, hours/cycles, optional lessor, the cabin
   seat layout, the cabin equipment list and the latest work orders of each airframe.
2. work_orders (10 docs) - the full maintenance history (checks, defect rectifications,
   component changes). Flat documents. Points to its aircraft and, for crew-reported defects,
   to the pilot who wrote the tech log entry.
3. crew_members (5 docs) - pilots (captains and first officers) with home address,
   spoken languages and licences/medical certificates.
4. flights (5 docs) - operated legs FCO-LHR-FCO, MXP-MAD-MXP and FCO-ATH, with the aircraft
   used, the operating pilots, the dispatch release, actual block times and passenger loads.
5. engine_trend_logs (5 docs) - engine condition monitoring: per engine and per flight, a
   group of time-ordered samples (N1, EGT, fuel flow, altitude, phase).

Patterns and how they work together
-----------------------------------
- Subset: aircraft.recent_work_orders[] keeps at most the 3 most recent work orders (by
  performed_at, newest first) copied unchanged from work_orders. AC-4101 has 4 work orders in
  the full collection and embeds only the latest 3 (the May 2026 A-check, WO-26-00412, is left
  out); the other aircraft have 2 work orders each and embed both.
- Referencing: work_orders.airframe -> aircraft (string _id "AC-410x");
  work_orders.reported_by -> crew_members (present in 6 of 10 work orders, absent for
  scheduled checks/hard-time changes); flights.pilots[] -> crew_members (list of 2 or 3
  ObjectIds, 3 on the MXP line-training rotation); flights.dispatch_release.accepted_by ->
  crew_members (the commander); engine_trend_logs.flight_id -> flights.
- ExtendedReference: flights.aircraft = {aircraft_id, registration, type_code}, copied from
  the aircraft document (many flights to one aircraft; AC-4101 and AC-4102 each fly two legs).
- Embedding: aircraft.seat_config (object), crew_members.home_address (object),
  crew_members.licences[] (array of objects), flights.dispatch_release (object),
  flights.block_times (object), flights.load_figures[] (array of objects, one per cabin).
- Array: aircraft.cabin_features[] (2-5 strings), crew_members.languages[] (2-3 codes).
- Bucket: engine_trend_logs.samples[] with window_start, window_end and sample_count; 2 to 6
  samples per document, sorted by recorded_at, all within the window and within the takeoff
  and landing times of the referenced flight.

Consistency notes: flight loads never exceed the seat_config of the aircraft flown; the same
engine serial (598871) appears on both legs flown by AC-4102 engine 1; reporters of work orders
are A320-family pilots only (the Embraer AC-4104 has no crew-reported work orders).

Difficulty requirements applied
-------------------------------
- Role-named identifiers: 4 of 6 identifier fields (airframe, reported_by, pilots,
  accepted_by); the other two are aircraft_id and flight_id.
- Mixed forms: single identifiers (work_orders.airframe, work_orders.reported_by,
  flights.dispatch_release.accepted_by, engine_trend_logs.flight_id) and a list of identifiers
  (flights.pilots[]); single embedded objects (aircraft.seat_config, crew_members.home_address,
  flights.dispatch_release, flights.block_times) and arrays of objects
  (crew_members.licences[], flights.load_figures[]).
- Prefixed string _id: aircraft ("AC-4101"), used with the same values in
  work_orders.airframe and flights.aircraft.aircraft_id.
- Collections with 2+ patterns other than Referencing: aircraft (Embedding, Array, Subset),
  crew_members (Embedding x2, Array), flights (ExtendedReference, Embedding x3).
- Pattern nested inside an Embedding: flights.dispatch_release.accepted_by (Referencing).
- Variable array lengths: cabin_features 2-5, recent_work_orders 2-3, languages 2-3,
  licences 2-3, pilots 2-3, load_figures 2-3, samples 2-6.
- Optional fields missing in some documents: aircraft.lessor, seat_config.business /
  premium_economy, crew_members.phone / training_role, licences[].expires_on,
  work_orders.ata_chapter / reported_by / mel_item, flights.gate / delay_minutes / delay_code,
  engine_trend_logs.data_source.
